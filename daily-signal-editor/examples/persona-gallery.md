# Persona Gallery

The same raw source items can produce different outputs depending on the user's job-to-be-done.

| Persona | Profile | Example Output | What Changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Founder | `mock-data/profiles/founder.json` | `examples/founder-brief.md` | Product opportunity, workflow moat, Korea go-to-market angle |
| B2B marketer | `mock-data/profiles/b2b-marketer.json` | `examples/b2b-marketer-brief.md` | Newsletter hooks, content narrative, customer education angle |
| Investor/analyst | `mock-data/profiles/investor-analyst.json` | `examples/investor-analyst-brief.md` | Thesis candidate, counter-signal, watchlist questions |
| Corporate strategist | `mock-data/profiles/corporate-strategist.json` | `examples/corporate-strategist-brief.md` | Strategy agenda, team update, competitive risk |

Use these examples to evaluate whether the skill is adapting the same information to different real-world workflows, rather than producing a generic news summary.
