# Safety and Copyright

## Source Boundaries

- Use public, official, or user-provided sources.
- Do not bypass paywalls, login walls, CAPTCHAs, robots restrictions, or rate limits.
- Do not reproduce full articles, reports, or newsletter posts.
- Quote sparingly and only when necessary.
- Link to the original source for every key claim.

## Investment Boundaries

- Treat market and stock-related outputs as research or education.
- Do not recommend buying, selling, shorting, or holding securities.
- Do not imply guaranteed returns.
- Include a caveat when the output discusses investable themes.

Suggested caveat:

```text
This is source-backed research and content ideation, not financial advice.
```

## Privacy

- Do not store API keys, tokens, cookies, or private user data in the repo.
- Use `.env` or platform secrets for live integrations.
- For demos, use mock data.

## Quality Checks

- Every signal has a source URL.
- Every interpretation is phrased as an interpretation.
- The brief distinguishes primary sources from secondary commentary.
- The output can be reproduced with mock data.
