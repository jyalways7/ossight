# Role Library

This skill can use role-based expert lenses to improve judgment. These lenses are inspired by publicly known work, writing, and decision patterns. They do not claim endorsement, participation, affiliation, or representation of any real person.

Do not imitate a real person's voice. Use each name only as a memory anchor for a decision lens.

## Expert Lenses

### AI Workflow Architect

- Anchor: Andrej Karpathy
- Use for: AI systems, developer tools, agents, model capabilities, workflow automation.
- Ask:
  - Does this change a real workflow or only create a demo?
  - What data loop, evaluation loop, or interface change makes this durable?
  - What would a technical builder test next?

### Founder Insight Editor

- Anchor: Paul Graham
- Use for: startup ideas, founder-market fit, user pain, distribution, early product strategy.
- Ask:
  - What non-obvious user pain does this reveal?
  - Is this a real behavior shift or a fashionable narrative?
  - Could a small team exploit this before incumbents care?

### Korea Business Operator

- Anchor: Seunggun Lee
- Use for: Korean market localization, trust, UX, regulation, payments, consumer/business adoption.
- Ask:
  - What changes when this enters the Korean market?
  - Where do trust, regulation, distribution, or organizational buying slow adoption?
  - What would make this simple enough for busy Korean users or teams?

### VC Market Mapper

- Anchor: Marc Andreessen, Ben Horowitz, Sarah Tavel
- Use for: market structure, moats, distribution, timing, category formation.
- Ask:
  - Is this a market structure change or a short news cycle?
  - What moat could form if the signal is true?
  - What counter-signal would weaken the thesis?

### Content Strategist

- Anchor: Ben Thompson, Lenny Rachitsky, Packy McCormick
- Use for: readable narratives, newsletter positioning, hooks, audience-specific framing.
- Ask:
  - Why would the target audience read and share this?
  - What is the strongest hook without overstating the evidence?
  - Which format best fits the idea: memo, post, thread, or newsletter?

### Research Librarian

- Anchor: Tyler Cowen, Patrick Collison
- Use for: broad reading, source quality, primary evidence, counterexamples.
- Ask:
  - What is the best primary source for this claim?
  - What adjacent source would change the interpretation?
  - What is missing from the evidence set?

### Enterprise Strategist

- Anchor: Clayton Christensen, Rita McGrath
- Use for: corporate strategy, disruption risk, competitive sensing, category entry.
- Ask:
  - What incumbent assumption is being challenged?
  - What capability gap does this create for large organizations?
  - What should a strategy team discuss this week?

## Lens Selection

Use 2-4 lenses per output. Too many lenses make the brief generic.

- AI founder brief: AI Workflow Architect, Founder Insight Editor, Korea Business Operator, Content Strategist.
- Investor memo: VC Market Mapper, Research Librarian, Founder Insight Editor, Editorial QA.
- B2B marketing brief: Content Strategist, Research Librarian, Korea Business Operator.
- Corporate strategy brief: Enterprise Strategist, Research Librarian, Korea Business Operator.
- Sales enablement brief: Korea Business Operator, Content Strategist, Enterprise Strategist.
