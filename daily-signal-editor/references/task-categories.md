# Task Categories

Use task categories to map a broad user request to a focused workflow.

## Categories

| Category | User Need | Best Outputs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source Discovery | Find high-quality sources for a domain | Source map, watchlist, feed list |
| Signal Scoring | Prioritize many candidate items | Ranked signals, score table |
| Pattern Synthesis | Connect multiple signals into a directional insight | Executive read, market thesis, contrarian view |
| Founder Strategy | Turn signals into product or market opportunities | Product angle, customer pain, positioning note |
| AI Workflow Analysis | Understand how AI changes work, product, or developer behavior | Workflow impact memo, implementation questions |
| Social Trend Sensing | Read weak signals from X, Threads, YouTube trending, search trends, and communities | Segment interest map, meme/content hook, weak-signal watchlist |
| App Ranking Analysis | Track rising apps, category shifts, and product patterns | App ranking readout, product idea list, category opportunity memo |
| Physical World Sensing | Read signals from spaces, objects, routes, queues, reservations, map reviews, and local commerce | Physical world brief, scene map, space/product/content angles |
| Spatial Strategy | Interpret why a place works, where it should exist, and whether the format can repeat | Location thesis, route analysis, operating-system note |
| Lifestyle Pattern Analysis | Turn repeated offline behavior into hidden desire and market hypotheses | Lifestyle thesis, object/ritual map, brand opportunity memo |
| Content/IP Opportunity | Translate media, creator, fandom, and entertainment signals into business ideas | IP extension ideas, creator strategy, content calendar |
| Korea Localization | Translate global signals into Korean market implications | Korea go-to-market notes, regulation/distribution checks |
| Investing Research | Build research-only market themes | Theme memo, watch questions, counter-signals |
| Content Packaging | Convert insights into publishable material | Newsletter, LinkedIn/X post, blog outline |
| Team Enablement | Share concise context with a team | Slack update, meeting agenda, sales talking points |
| Editorial QA | Check evidence, copyright, and overclaiming | Source audit, caveat list, publication checklist |

## Routing

- Founder or operator requests: activate `Founder Strategy`, `AI Workflow Analysis`, `Korea Localization`, and `Content Packaging`.
- Investor or analyst requests: activate `Signal Scoring`, `Pattern Synthesis`, `Investing Research`, and `Editorial QA`.
- Content requests: activate `Source Discovery`, `Pattern Synthesis`, `Content Packaging`, and `Editorial QA`.
- Broad trend requests: activate `Social Trend Sensing`, `App Ranking Analysis`, `Content/IP Opportunity`, `Pattern Synthesis`, and `Editorial QA`.
- Material-world, space, retail, local business, or lifestyle requests: activate `Physical World Sensing`, `Spatial Strategy`, `Lifestyle Pattern Analysis`, `Korea Localization`, `Pattern Synthesis`, and `Editorial QA`.
- Corporate strategy requests: activate `Signal Scoring`, `Korea Localization`, `Team Enablement`, and `Editorial QA`.
- Sales requests: activate `Pattern Synthesis`, `Korea Localization`, `Team Enablement`, and `Content Packaging`.
